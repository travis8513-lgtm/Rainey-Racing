The Season, Day by Day

Apr 12
Camden Speedway · Opening Day

🥈 Season Opener — Travis Runs 2nd in Box Stock

  • 🥈 Travis #10X — Box Stock: Started 2nd and finished 2nd in the heat, then backed it up with another 2nd-place run in the feature. The kart ran good for the opening day of the season.

A solid start to 2026 — and a day that changed the team. Watching Travis run up front is what inspired Ronnie to get a kart of his own that very next weekend. That gave Dad just one week to get the new kart race-ready for Ronnie's debut on April 26. He got it done — because that's what this family does.

Apr 26
Camden Speedway

🏆 Travis Sweeps Box Stock — Ronnie Grabs a Podium in His Debut

  • 🥇 Travis #10X — Box Stock: A perfect day — started P1 and won the heat, started P1 and won the feature. The kart ran good all day long.
  • 🏁 Ronnie #3 — Clone Class (debut): First-ever race day in his brand-new kart, with zero practice laps before the green flag — started 4th and finished 4th in the heat.
  • 🥈 Ronnie #3 — Clone Class feature: Started 4th and charged to a 2nd-place finish — a podium in his very first race day.

Ronnie's debut had all the drama. During intermission, Travis took Ronnie's new kart out to shake it down — and about three laps in, the chain broke. The team got it back together, sent it out for one more lap, and then the motor plate came loose and the chain popped off again. With the clock running out before the feature, there was only one call to make: Ronnie jumped into Travis's kart, started 4th in the Clone Class feature, and drove it all the way up to 2nd. A brand-new driver, a borrowed kart, and a podium — that's one heck of a first day.

Jun 28
Camden Speedway

🏆 Travis Sweeps Box Stock — Brothers Finish 3rd & 4th in RWYB

  • 🥇 Travis #10X — Box Stock: Won the heat AND the feature — a perfect day on a brand-new box-stock motor. Kart ran great all day.
  • 🏁 Travis #10X — Run What You Brung: Took the lead in the heat until getting pushed out of his groove, then a shirt wrapped in the clutch ended his heat halfway in. The team got it cleared in time for the feature — and he charged from 7th up to 4th.
  • 🥉 Ronnie #3 — Run What You Brung: 4th in the heat with the kart running great, then a podium — 3rd in the feature. The kart was pushing in the feature, so some tire adjustments are on the list before the next race.

Best part of the day: the brothers took the checkered flag nose to tail — Ronnie in 3rd with Travis in 4th right on his bumper and closing every lap. Both karts performed great and rolled right back onto the trailer with no major work needed. And after all the rain that kept us off the track, it sure felt good to be back racing. A feature win, a heat win, and a podium in one Sunday — that's Rainey Racing.

Jul 18
Adjoining Hearts Raceway · Woodlawn, TN

🆕 New Track, New Lessons — Test & Tune Under the Lights

  • 🚚 Road Trip: We loaded up the 10X and the 3 and made the haul to a brand-new track — Adjoining Hearts Raceway in Woodlawn, TN — for a night of test & tune and racing under the lights.
  • 🔧 Ronnie #3 — Dialed In: A few tire adjustments transformed his kart — it quit pushing and started driving the way we've been chasing all year. Big step forward for the 3.
  • 🏁 Travis #10X — Flat Out: By the end of the night the track came in so good Travis could hold the throttle wide open the whole way around — so hard the carburetor was running out of fuel! He and Ronnie ran a few laps side by side, too.
  • 📈 What We Learned: We rolled in thinking we were ready and found out real quick we don't have the motor to run with those guys just yet. Now we know exactly what to work on before we go back.

Not every night is about the trophy — some are about getting better, and this was one of those. Adjoining Hearts is a first-class little bullring: well-prepped, fast, and just plain fun to race on. The biggest thanks go to the group of racers who run over there — they welcomed us, helped us out, and taught us a bunch in a single night. We've got some adjustments to make before we can run up front with them, but we'll be back — because that's how you get better. 🏁

Aug 1
Sleepy Hollow Speedway · Indian Mound, TN

🆕 Practice Night at Sleepy Hollow — The Gear Change Paid Off

  • 🚚 Another New Track: We loaded up the 10X and the 3 and headed to Sleepy Hollow Speedway in Indian Mound, TN for a practice night — one more new place to learn.
  • 🏁 The Track: Sleepy Hollow was great all night long — a fun little dirt track that gave us plenty to work with.
  • ⚙️ The Gear Change: We changed gears on both karts, and that turned out to be the ticket — the 10X and the 3 both ran great the rest of the night.
  • 😃 Bottom Line: A lot of fun and an all-around great practice night for the team.

Nights like this are what build a season — no points on the line, just seat time on a new track and a chance to get the karts dialed in. Once we made that gear change both karts came alive, and we had a blast running laps under the lights. The biggest thank you goes to the sponsors who make it possible for us to load up and try out new tracks like this one — Bubba's Hot Dogs, Pouring Rein Mobile, and Brandi Bath at BBB Creations. Thank y'all so much for your support. 🏁

Aug 9
Camden Speedway

🥈 Two Podiums for the 10X — Chain Trouble Sidelines the 3

  • 🥈 Travis #10X — Box Stock: 2nd in the heat and 2nd in the feature. Solid, consistent speed all day long.
  • 🥉 Travis #10X — Run What You Brung: 3rd in the heat and 3rd in the feature — a second podium to go with the first.
  • 🥉 Ronnie #3 — Clone: 3rd in the heat race, and the 3 was rolling good early.
  • 🔧 Ronnie #3 — Chain Trouble: Chain issues hung around all program long and kept the 3 out of both the Clone and RWYB features.

A day of two halves. The 10X put together four straight top-three runs and came home with two podiums — we're knocking on the door of that top step, and every lap and every adjustment gets us closer. The 3 wasn't so lucky: a chain problem we couldn't get ahead of ended the day early and cost us both features. But we don't make excuses — we find the problem, fix it, and come back stronger. We know what needs doing, and the 3 will be ready to go back to battle at the next race. 🔧💪

📸 A big shoutout to RE Photography for the great photos and video from race day. Because of everything we were fighting with the 3, she didn't get as many shots of that kart as we'd have liked — we're looking forward to having her back and getting plenty of both karts next time.

🙏 And a special thank you to our 2026 sponsors — Bubba's Hot Dogs, Brandi Bath at BBB Creations & DTF Prints, and Pouring Rein Mobile. Your support keeps these karts on the track. Above all, we give God the glory for another day doing what we love, surrounded by family, friends, and supporters. West Tennessee built. Family driven. God first. 💜💛🏁

Aug 23
Camden Speedway

🥈 Chain Breaks on the Pole — Then a Charge From the Rear

  • 🚩 Travis #10X — Box Stock Heat: Started on the pole and ran up front about four laps before the chain broke. Day was off to a rough start.
  • 🔧 Travis #10X — RWYB Heat: The team couldn't get the chain back on in time, so he had to sit that one out too — both heats gone.
  • 🥈 Travis #10X — Box Stock Feature: Chain back on and shaken down, he rolled off dead last and drove all the way up to 2nd.
  • 🏁 Travis #10X — RWYB Feature: Started the rear again and came home 4th, right on Ronnie's bumper at the flag.
  • 🥈 Ronnie #3 — Clone: Started 4th and finished 2nd in the heat, then started 2nd and finished 2nd in the feature.
  • 🥉 Ronnie #3 — Run What You Brung: Started 4th and finished 3rd in the heat, then started 3rd and finished 3rd in the feature.

Four laps in, leading the Box Stock heat from the pole, and the chain broke. By the time the team got it sorted, both of Travis's heat races were gone. So they put it back together, ran a couple of laps before the features just to be sure it would stay on — and then he started dead last in both features and finished 2nd and 4th. Ronnie was the steady one all day: 2nd and 3rd in his heats, then a 2nd in Clone and a 3rd in Run What You Brung. And once again the brothers came across nose to tail in RWYB, Ronnie 3rd with Travis right behind him trying to run him down — Travis will tell you straight, Ronnie was just flat faster. After the heats the track rubbered down and you could race all over it, top to bottom. Four features, four top-four finishes, and a broken chain that didn't get the last word. 🏁

🎥 Watch both features: we got the Box Stock and Clone races on video. The one race we both ran in — the Run What You Brung feature — is the one we didn't get recorded. Figures! 😅

Sep 4
Rainey Racing Shop

🆕 New Kart Day — Say Hello to the #14

  • 🏎️ The Kart: A Phantom chassis — and it's already yellow, like it was meant to be. Came home Friday night on the trailer with a stack of spare tires and wheels.
  • 🔢 The Number: It'll run as the #14 with Travis behind the wheel in Box Stock.
  • 🔧 What It Means for the 10X: The 10X moves to Run What You Brung full-time — Dad's building the engine and putting performance parts on it.
  • 🏁 First Time Out: Sunday, September 13 at Camden Speedway.

Three karts on the trailer now. This one flat would not have happened without our sponsors — Bubba's Hot Dogs, Pouring Rein Mobile, and Brandi Bath at BBB Creations all pitched in to help us get it, and we can't thank y'all enough. These pictures are from the night we brought it home, still wearing the old owner's 22 and stickers — keep an eye out for it as the #14. Come see it Sunday. 🏁

Sep 13
Camden Speedway

🆕 The #14 Phantom Debuts — Two Podiums on Day One

  • 🥉 Travis #14 — Box Stock: Started on the pole but the kart pushed bad in the corners and the gearing wasn't right, so he finished 3rd in the heat. After a gear change it was a little faster, and he finished 3rd in the feature.
  • 🥉 Ronnie #3 — Clone: Started on the pole of the heat and finished 3rd. Started 3rd in the feature and finished 3rd.
  • 🏁 Travis #10X — RWYB: Started 4th and was running 5th when engine trouble hit with 3 laps to go in the heat. Another racer loaned him his backup kart so he could run the feature for points, and he finished 4th.
  • 🏁 Ronnie #3 — RWYB: Started 4th in the feature and finished 5th.

Debut day for the Phantom — still wearing the previous owner's #22, since the new #14 numbers didn't go on until Sept 27. We had issues with both of Travis's karts, but we came home with two 3rd-place finishes and a list of exactly what to fix. Ronnie's 3 ran good all day long. And a big thank you to the racer who let Travis borrow his backup kart to keep his points going. 🏁

Sep 27
Camden Speedway

🔧 New Numbers, New Motor — and a Long Day of Lessons

  • 🎨 The #14 Phantom: Rolled out wearing its new #14 numbers and our sponsor logos.
  • 🏁 Travis #14 — Box Stock: Started 5th and finished 4th in the heat. A gear change before the feature turned out to be the wrong direction. Started 4th and finished 4th.
  • 💨 Travis #10X — RWYB: Debuted a new-to-us motor with a lot of horsepower. Started on the pole and battled for the lead for two laps, but it started losing power and the exhaust fell off right after the flag stand on the last lap. In the feature he started 4th, but the motor wouldn't run right and was losing fuel, so he had to pull off.
  • 🚫 Ronnie #3: Trouble from the moment we unloaded. It wouldn't run, and we broke three pull ropes trying. No racing for Ronnie this day.

Between the exhaust, two backed-out valve cover bolts, gearing on the 14 and a 3 kart that just wouldn't fire, this one tested us. But we learned a lot about all three karts and what needed fixing before the next race. 🏁

Camden Speedway

🌧️ Last Points Race Rained Out

Mother Nature had the final say on the last points night. No racing, so everything came down to the season finale on October 25.

Two Rivers Bar & Grill

🎃 Candy for the Kids at Two Rivers

Oct 25
Camden Speedway · Season Finale & Awards

🏆 Travis Wins the Box Stock Championship

  • 🏆 Travis — Box Stock Points: 2026 Box Stock Champion! — 294 points, ahead of 2nd place at 275
  • 🥉 Travis — RWYB Points: 3rd place in Run What You Brung.
  • 🏅 Ronnie — Points: 4th in Clone and 4th in Run What You Brung, plus a participation trophy.
  • 🚩 Travis #14 — Box Stock: Started on the pole and fell back to 4th in the heat, with no brakes. A brake line got caught under the tire and rubbed a hole in it, so he couldn't run the feature.
  • ⛽ Ronnie #3: Started on the pole of his heat and fell back to 4th before a fuel problem ended his race. Started 4th in the feature and fuel problems struck again.
  • ⛓️ Travis #10X — RWYB: Another new-to-us motor, but the chain broke the moment he pulled on for hot laps. Another racer loaned him his backup kart. He started 3rd in the heat and was running good until he got high, hit the berm on the back straightaway, spun, and the chain came off. He helped fix the kart, started the feature from the rear and finished 4th.

Finale day didn't go easy. We had to make changes to the 14 and the 3 before racing even started, and then it was brakes, fuel and chains all day long. But when the points were counted, Travis was the 2026 Box Stock champion and 3rd in Run What You Brung, and Ronnie came home 4th in both Clone and RWYB. A big thank you to the racer who loaned Travis his backup kart. That's what this sport is about. It was a great season with plenty of ups and downs, and we'll be fighting even harder in 2027. Thank you to our sponsors, our family and everyone who came out to cheer. All glory to God. 💜💛🏁
